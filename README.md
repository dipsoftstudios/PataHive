# PataHive — Android Productivity & Personal Organizer App 🐝

**PataHive is an Android productivity app for organizing notes, bookmarks, shopping lists, watchlists, flashcards, and documents in one place.** Use it as a personal organizer to collect useful links, remember everyday tasks, keep study cards together, and manage PDFs and images.

## Features

- 🔖 **Bookmark manager** — Save links with titles, descriptions, and tags.
- 📝 **Notes app** — Capture ideas and reminders, and keep audio recordings with your notes.
- 🛒 **Shopping list** — Organize items you need to buy.
- 🎬 **Watchlist** — Keep track of movies and shows you want to watch.
- 🧠 **Flashcards** — Create cards to support studying and revision.
- 📄 **Document organizer** — Keep PDFs and image documents together.
- 🖼️ **Image editor for documents** — Crop, rotate, and apply document-style filters to images.
- 📑 **Image-to-PDF converter** — Combine multiple images into a multi-page PDF, or save an image document as PDF, JPG, or PNG.

## Share to PataHive

Use Android’s share menu to send supported items to PataHive. Save links to Bookmarks, PDFs and images to Documents, and audio recordings to Notes.

PataHive brings everyday productivity tools together in one personal organizer, helping you keep useful information easy to find.
